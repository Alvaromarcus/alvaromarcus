I'm a software developer with a background in Electrical Engineering and a
master's degree in Control & Automation. I work at the intersection of
**software**, **embedded systems**, and **artificial intelligence**, from
full-stack web applications to microcontroller firmware and computer vision
models for industry.

## What shows up here

- **Operational efficiency** — consulting and diagnostics for the industrial sector.
- **Embedded systems** — hardware and firmware (STM32, ESP32, CAN bus).
- **AI and computer vision** — YOLO models for industrial monitoring.
- **Aeromodeling and drones** — builds, designs, and setups.

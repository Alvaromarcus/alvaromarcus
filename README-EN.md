### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a software developer with a background in Electrical Engineering and a Master's degree in Control & Automation. I work at the intersection of **software**, **embedded systems**, and **artificial intelligence**, with hands-on experience in projects ranging from full-stack web applications to microcontroller firmware and computer vision models for industry.

- 🔭 Currently working with **TypeScript**, **Python**, and **embedded systems**
- 🤖 Experience with **YOLO**, **MLP neural networks**, and **computer vision** applied to industry
- ⚡ Strong background in **STM32**, **ESP32**, and industrial protocols (**CAN Bus**)
- 📝 I write about tech on my [blog](https://alvaromarcus.github.io/alvaromarcus/)
